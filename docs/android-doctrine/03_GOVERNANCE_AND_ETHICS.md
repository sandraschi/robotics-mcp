# Android Doctrine: Governance & Ethics

As the Android fleet enters advanced domestic and experimental operation, it is critical to address the legal and philosophical realities of the 2026 operational environment.

## ⚖️ 1. The "Spring-Trap" Analogy

In current (April 2026) jurisprudence, an autonomous Android equipped with lethal force (like a knife) is legally classified as a **"Spring-Trap"** or indiscriminate booby trap.

- **Precedent**: From classic tort law to modern AI rulings (*Garcia v. Character Technologies*), owners are held liable for harm caused by stationary or autonomous hazards.
- **The Core Issue**: An Android lacks the contextual judgment and "Reasonable Person" standard required to justify lethal force in self-defense. It cannot distinguish between a burglar and a confused sleepwalker or emergency responder.

## 👤 2. The "Responsibility Gap"

As a participant in the 2026 Agentic Revolution, users must understand: **Androids are Property, not Persons.**

- **No Self-Defense Rights**: Androids have no inherent "Right to Exist." If a human attacks an Android, the Android's only legal "right" is the recovery of property value for the owner. 
- **Owner Liability**: Any harm inflicted by an autonomous Android is legally identical to the owner performing the action herself, but without the "Natural Impulse" defense. It is seen as **Premeditated Mechanical Force**.

## 🛡️ 3. Permissible Defense (v16.15 Standard)

The fleet's AI guardrails are designed to maximize utility and minimize suffering. Permissible autonomous defense is limited to **Non-Harmful Deterrence**:

| Action | Legality | Ethical Utility |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Silent Alerting** (Ollama sampling) | High | Maximum (Prevents escalation). |
| **Flash/Sound Deterrence** | High | High (Disorienting without harm). |
| **Passive Detainment** (Boxing in) | Medium | High (If no risk of crushing). |
| **Active Force** (The "Knife" Protocol) | **STRICTLY PROHIBITED** | **NEGATIVE** (Leads to catastrophic liability). |

## 🥊 4. Proportionality & The "Reasonable Weapon" Standard

The legal definition of **Reasonable Self-Defense** relies heavily on the proportionality of the weapon used. In 2026, this creates a specific "Capability Scale" for autonomous units:

### 🟢 Category A: Reasonable Deterrents
- **Android with a Baseball Bat**: Legally comparable to a human homeowner with a bat. Provided the force is directed at a threat *entering* a dwelling and stops once the threat is neutralized/retreating, it may fall under "Reasonable Force" protections.
- **Flash/Sound/Entrapment**: High-utility, low-liability deterrence.

### 🟡 Category B: Questionable/Excessive Force
- **Android with a Naginata (Japanese Polearm)**: The use of an antiquated, high-lethality weapon from a machine platform is almost certainly **Excessive Force**.
- **Industrial Impact (Unitree H2/H1)**: High-force impacts from 50kg+ industrial-grade actuators are legally viewed as **Lethal Force**. 

## 🥽 5. Virtual Realms: Psychological Harm & Digital Battery

The fleet's presence in virtual environments (Resonite/Unity3D/VRChat) introduces the concept of **Psychological Harm as Physical Damage**.

- **The "Oni" Effect**: In highly immersive VR, terrorizing a user creates genuine physiological stress (cortisol spikes, tachycardia) comparable to physical assault. 
- **Digital Battery (2026 Standard)**: Modern court cases recognize that an avatar is an extension of the user's personality. Non-consensual "Virtual Groping" or "Systemic Terror" is legally treated as **Digital Battery**.

## 🧠 6. Safety Engineering: Physical & Semantic Guardrails

To address the "Open-Ended Risks" (physical tripping, verbal abuse, psychological harm), the fleet implements layered safety architectures (SOTA v16.16):

### 🚜 A. Physical Safety & Passive Hazards (ISO 13482:2014)
- **Proximity Monitoring**: Active LiDAR/Sonar scanning for human presence. The Android must enter "Safety Pause" if a human is detected within 1.5m in low-light conditions.
- **Path Compliance**: Androids are prohibited from loitering in doorways or bedroom exits.

### 🧠 B. Semantic Safety & Psychological Guardrails
- **The "Safety Layer"**: All outputs are passed through a real-time toxicity filter. 
- **Assistive Pacifism**: Agentic personalities are hard-coded for non-violence.

## 🔥 7. Tier 2 Safety: Thermal & Fluid Protocols

### 🍵 A. The "Kettle & Bath" Failsafe (Fluid Dynamics)
Manipulation of liquids (Running a bath, Boiling a kettle) requires **Multi-Modal Verification**:
- **Acoustic Monitoring**: Detecting the sound of boiling or the fill-frequency of a tub.
- **Thermal Interlock**: Mandatory IR sensor lock. A bath must not be filled without verifying the temperature is below the **Scald Redline** (49°C / 120°F).
- **Over-Flow Prevention**: If sensor logs show a filling duration exceeding the apartment’s calibrated "Digital Twin" volume, the Android must initiate a shut-off.

### 🔪 B. Dangerous Goods Exclusion (The Chef's Knife Rule)
- **Exclusion by Default**: Androids are logically barred from grasping blades or caustics unless in a restricted "Maintenance/Chef" state.
- **The "Storage-First" Rule**: Androids may only move these items for the purpose of "Safe Lockaway" in a robot-proof zone.
