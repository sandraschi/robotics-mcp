# Android Doctrine: The Social Acceptance Vector

As the fleet expands into **External Logistics (SPAR Missions)** and public hallways, the "Social Acceptance" of the Android is as critical as its torque-output.

## 🕶️ 1. The "Non-Invasive Observer" Protocol

To avoid the "Creepy Robot" stigma, the fleet utilizes a **Passive-First** social approach:

### 🔇 A. The "Acoustic Footprint"
- **Requirement**: No sudden high-decibel motor whines in public spaces.
- **The Whisper Standard**: Stepper motors and gearboxes must be dampened to <45dB during sidewalk navigation.
- **Social Logic**: A quiet robot is a "Neutral Object." A noisy robot is a "Active Threat."

### 👁️ B. Visual Gaze & Privacy
- **Requirement**: The Android's primary camera (Gimbal) must not persistently track human faces in public.
- **Gaze Law**: The gimbal should prioritize **Path Clearance (Low)** and **Environmental Markers (High)**. 
- **Privacy Standard**: Onboard face-recognition must be processed in the **Enclave (Local-only)**. No facial biometric data should ever leave the unit's local RAM during public interaction.

## 🤝 2. Public Interaction Dynamics

### 🛑 A. Path Supremacy
- **Rule**: Humans ALWAYS have the right of way. 
- **Action**: In a hallway conflict, the Android must move to the **Right-most wall** and enter "Yield State" (Statue Mode) until the human has passed.
- **Reasoning**: This signals Predictability. A robot that tries to "dodge" humans creates unpredictable collision-dance tension.

### 🏗️ B. The "Utility" Aesthetic
- **Design Logic**: The Android should look like a **Tool**, not a "Replica Human."
- **Visual Cues**: Use high-visibility safety markers on the chassis for external missions. 
- **The Shopper Standard**: When on a grocery mission, the presence of the **Passive Trolley** immediately contextualizes the robot's purpose to onlookers, lowering social resistance.

## 🏛️ 3. The Neighborhood "Vibe" Integration

### 🏡 A. The "Good Neighbor" Policy
- **Logic**: The fleet must contribute to the household's "Vibe" by performing invisible maintenance (path sweeping, lighting adjustment).
- **Social Contract**: A robot that "helps" becomes a valued neighbor. A robot that "watches" becomes a pariah.

### 🛡️ B. Hazard Mitigation as Public Relations
- **Action**: If the Android detects a leak or fire in a neighbor's vicinity, it must initiate a **Silent Alert** to the owner immediately.
- **Governance**: However, active "Prying" (recording audio/video through neighbor windows) is **STRICTLY PROHIBITED**.
