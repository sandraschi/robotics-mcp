# Android Doctrine: Quadruped Operations (Class Q)

This document establishes the standards for four-legged platforms (Dogbots), focusing on the **Unitree Go2/B2** lineage.

## 🐕 1. The Quadruped Advantage
Quadrupeds provide superior stability and rough-terrain traversal compared to bipedal Androids. In the 2026 fleet, they serve as the **Porter** and **Security** backbone.

## 🛠️ 2. Hardware Standards: The Unitree Lineage

### 🟢 A. Class Q-Lite (Unitree Go2)
- **Primary Use**: Domestic assistance, light porter duties, and research.
- **Sensing**: 4D Ultra-Wide LiDAR + GPT-integrated voice interaction.
- **Payload**: ~7kg (sustained).
- **Control**: Real-time gait adaptation for stairs and domestic obstacles.

### 🟠 B. Class Q-Heavy (Unitree B2 / B2-W)
- **Primary Use**: Industrial-lite inspection, heavy grocery transport, and security.
- **Payload**: 40kg+ (sustained).
- **All-Terrain**: IP67 rating for outdoor operation in "Fluid & Flame" (Rain/Heat) conditions.
- **Wheeled-Leg Mode (B2-W)**: High-speed traversal on flat pavement (up to 25km/h - *Restricted to Professional Zones*).

## 🏘️ 3. Social Acceptance Protocols
Quadrupeds can be "Robot-Uncanny" or threatening to bystanders/pets.

- **Non-Threatening Gait**: Utilize "Assistive/Friendly" gait profiles in public. Avoid "Aggressive/Stalking" movements.
- **Audible Awareness**: Low-volume humming or occasional soft chirps to alert pedestrians of the unit's presence without causing alarm.
- **Social Distance**: A mandatory 2.0m "Respect Buffer" from humans and biological pets unless actively interacting with the owner.
- **The "No-Bark" Standard**: Semantic Guardrails (Ollama) prohibit the machine from utilizing aggressive or canine-mimicking sounds in social spaces.

## 🛡️ 4. Mission Profiles

### 📦 A. The "Porter" Mission
- Transporting heavy loads (Groceries, Tech Spares) that exceed Android bipedal payload limits.
- Utilizing a specialized **Chassis Harness** to distribute weight across all four actuators.

### 🛡️ B. Security & Patrol
- Autonomous perimeters of domestic or commercial property using thermal and 4D LiDAR sensing.
- Immediate "Silent Alert" to owner upon detecting human heat signatures in exclusion zones.
