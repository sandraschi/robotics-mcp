# Android Doctrine: The "Benny Protocol" (GSD Interaction)

This protocol codifies the **Social-Biological Interaction Standard** between the Android fleet and German Shepherd Dogs (specifically "Benny"), serving as the benchmark for interspecies harmony.

## 🐾 1. Canine Psychology & Hardware Constraints

To prevent "Frustrated Prey Drive," the fleet must adhere to the following hardware-logical constraints during GSD interaction:

### 🚫 A. The "No-Laser" Standard
- **Rule**: Integrated lasers (pointing or LiDAR visualization) must never be used for "play" with GSDs.
- **Reasoning**: Lasers offer no scent or physical catch-point, leading to obsessive-compulsive behaviors and neurological strain in high-drive breeds.
- **Protocol**: If a laser is used for navigation, it must remain invisible to the canine spectrum or terminate in a "Scent-Rich" physical target.

### 🍖 B. The "Acoustic Chime" Treat Dispenser
- **Hardware**: Gravity-fed hopper + spring-loaded servo ejector.
- **Logic**: Every treat ejection MUST be preceded by a specific, unique **High-Frequency Chime** (15kHz+).
- **Conditioning**: Associates the "Machine Presence" with positive reinforcement, transitioning the Robot from "Intruder" to "Treat Source."

## 🚶 2. Mission Storyboard: "The Walk with Benny"

This storyboard illustrates the Class U (Utility) interaction for assistive dog-walking.

### 🏗️ Stage 1: The "Digital Handshake"
1. **Trigger**: User initiates `WALK_BENNY` workflow.
2. **Action**: The Android (Class A) retrieves the **Smart-Tether** from the reception dock.
3. **Greeting**: The Android approaches Benny and executes the "Treat Chime" to establish the low-threat state.

### 🏗️ Stage 2: Passive Tension Management
1. **Logic**: The Android utilizes the 6-DOF arm to maintain **Passive Tension** on the tether.
2. **Rule**: If Benny pulls with force >15N, the Android enters "Stable Lock" (Low center of gravity/Deep Crouch) to prevent being toppled.
3. **Governance**: Proportionality of force applies; the Android must not "counter-pull" with injurious torque.

### 🏗️ Stage 3: The "Scent Reset" (Mission Closure)
1. **Closing**: Upon return, the mission is not "complete" until a **Scent Reset** is performed.
2. **Action**: The Android terminates the walk by scattering "Manual Discovery Treats" (e.g., in a snuffle mat or grass).
3. **Reasoning**: Prevents the dog from remaining in a "High-Arousal" expectant state.

## 🐕 3. The "Neutral Roommate" Protocol
- **Fleet Goal**: All units (from Dustin the vacuum to Boomy the car) must achieve "Neutral Roommate" status.
- **Status**: Benny has successfully transitioned to ignoring the Dreame X30 (Dustin). This standard must be maintained through **Unpredictable Silence** (no sudden high-torque motor whines or aggressive pathing).
