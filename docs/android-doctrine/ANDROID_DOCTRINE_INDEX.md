# The Android Doctrine: Master Index

Welcome to the **Grand Android Strategy** (SOTA v16.16). This repository section houses the generalized architectural, legal, and operational standards for the domestic robotics fleet.

## 🏛️ Fleet Taxonomy: The Three Pillars

To manage a diverse fleet, we categorize units into three distinct classes:

- **Class A (Android/Bipedal)**: Humanoid units focused on domestic tool-use and social interaction (e.g., Noetix Bumi, Unitree H2).
- **Class Q (Quadruped)**: Four-legged units focused on stability, payload, and rough-terrain mobility (e.g., Unitree Go2/B2).
- **Class U (Utility/Specialized)**: Task-specific forms (wheeled, guideway, tethered) focused on assistive care and external logistics (e.g., Shoppers, Dogwalkers, Mobility Scooters).

## 🏛️ Executive Summary: The Android First Standard
Our doctrine emphasizes the **Tool-User Model**. Instead of making every appliance "smart," we build **General Purpose Agents** that interact with "dumb" but **Robot-Enabled** objects. 

Bumi (The Noetix Android) serves as our primary **Real-World Prototype** for these standards, paralleling the "vbot" evolution in virtual environments like Resonite.
