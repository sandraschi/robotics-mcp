## 3. Physical Plausibility Gating

A purely neural world model can hallucinate non-physical slip or impossible penetrations. The fleet standard mandates **Analytical Gating**:
1. Every trajectory rollout generated by Genie 4 passes through the MuJoCo/Isaac contact verification gate.
2. The Variational Free Energy (VFE) anomaly $\Delta F$ is computed:
   $$\Delta F = \frac{1}{2} (x_{\text{world}} - x_{\text{physics}})^T \Pi_x (x_{\text{world}} - x_{\text{physics}})$$
3. If $\Delta F > \epsilon_{\text{divergence}}$, an active inference exploratory reflex is triggered. If $\Delta F > 1.5 \times \epsilon_{\text{divergence}}$, the hardware watchdog commands an immediate latching E-Stop.
